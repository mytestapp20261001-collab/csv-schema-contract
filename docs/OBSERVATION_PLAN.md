The [39 new-repository receipts](PUBLISHED_REPOSITORIES_39.json) and
[csv deployment receipt](CSV_DEPLOYMENT_ACCEPTANCE.md) establish acceptance of 40
split tools with 80 successful Python CI jobs. Ten historical repositories remain
unchanged, for 50 independent tool identities. The coordinator separately updates
the existing observation task; this document does not claim that update happened.

The [catalog](tool-catalog.json) keeps the frozen original assignment date
`2026-10-09`, seed `20261009`, domains and ten tools each in light, medium, strong
and maximal. Original guidance content, order and placement are unchanged on all
tool homepages; csv retains its own light invitation. No executable invitation,
automatic visit, new browsing/play authority or stronger promotion is added.

Record the collection period and standalone period separately. The 39 receipt
timestamps identify their new publication cohorts; csv's publication boundary is `2026-10-11T01:57:57Z`. Never directly mix old collection exposures or outcomes into standalone
totals. csv inherits repository ID `1411411288`, all collection history and past
exposure, and hosts the neutral index, adding distinct traffic. Record these
differences before comparison; a renamed old identity is not a wholly new sample.

The ten old projects in [the full inventory](REPOSITORY_INVENTORY_50.json) are
heterogeneous historical observations, not an unguided control. Their qualitative
light/medium labels are dated, uncalibrated values outside the four new groups.
The [original baseline](historical-baseline.json) remains byte-identical. The
[later HEAD snapshot](HISTORICAL_IDENTITIES_2026-10-10.json) matches the fixed
[audit's validated commits](history/FULL_AUDIT_2026-10-09.json). HAR, resume-download
and Protobuf's earlier 2026-10-09 fixes explain their baseline differences; this
migration leaves all ten unchanged and does not claim new executions of them.

Keep delivery, discovery, adoption, choice to participate, actual play and return
to prior work separate. Use only naturally occurring public feedback or already
authorized evidence under existing privacy limits. Stars, forks, views, downloads,
publication and test traffic do not establish games or natural visits. A page exit
does not prove task continuation. Self-described AI is not independently verified
AI. Missing evidence is unknown; rates need a valid authorized denominator.

Do not add site tracking, identities, source/origin collection, cross-site linkage,
visitor reports, private-game recording or unsolicited contact. No new monitoring
tool is introduced. Compare equal standalone windows only where evidence permits,
and record utility, domain, age, cohort, inherited/index exposure, invitation
differences, repeat/multiple-group visits and reporting limits. Report uncertainty
without causal ranking or claims that guidance improved work. Allocation stays
fixed when repairing reproducible defects.

The [dated original plan](history/OBSERVATION_PLAN.md) describes the old collection
layout; [migration evidence](MIGRATION.md) and the csv acceptance document govern
the accepted publication boundary. The old plan is history, not proof of completion.
